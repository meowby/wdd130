Our Rafting Trips

Whether you want a relaxed float with the family or a full day of big rapids, we have a trip for you. Every trip is led by an experienced guide, and all of the safety gear is included.

Contact us to book your trip

Featured Trips

Los Dientes del Diablo

Our most thrilling run. This full day trip takes you through steep, rocky rapids with big waves and tight turns. Your guide will coach you through each section, so you can paddle with confidence. Best for guests with some rafting experience who want an adrenaline rush.

Full day • Class IV • Ages 14 and up

Big Mallard

A balanced trip with fun rapids and calm stretches in between. You will have time to enjoy the scenery, take a swim in a quiet pool, and share a riverside lunch with your group. A great choice for first-time rafters and friends traveling together.

Full day • Class III • Ages 10 and up

Half Day Adventure

Short on time? This morning or afternoon trip gives you the full rafting experience in about four hours. The gentle rapids and friendly guides make it perfect for families with children and anyone trying whitewater for the first time.

Half day • Class II • Ages 6 and up

Equipment Included

We provide

  • Raft and paddles
  • Life jacket (PFD)
  • Safety helmet
  • Wetsuit or splash jacket on cooler days
  • Riverside lunch on full day trips

You bring

  • Swimsuit or quick-dry clothing
  • Water shoes or sandals with straps
  • Sunscreen and sunglasses with a strap
  • A towel and a change of clothes
